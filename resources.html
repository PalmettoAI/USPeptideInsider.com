Things We Actually Use

This page is the short list of things we use ourselves and have recommended in person to friends and family before we ever wrote a word about them publicly.

It is intentionally short. A long list would be marketing. We will only ever list things here that we have used personally, that meet a real bar, and that we would recommend without compensation. Where compensation exists, we say so.

This page is currently a placeholder. We have not yet built out the full resource list — when we do, every entry will follow the standard:

  • What it is, in one sentence.
  • Why we recommend it, in 50–80 words.
  • What it costs.
  • Whether the link is an affiliate link (some will be, some won't).
  • What we'd recommend instead if it isn't right for you.

Categories we plan to cover:

  • Sleep tracking (the device we wear)
  • Direct-to-consumer lab testing (the panels we use, per Letter #3)
  • Supplements (the short list of things with real evidence — most of what's on Instagram is not on this list)
