[ 04 / EDITORIAL ]

How we evaluate sourcing

Every vendor in our database is held to the same six-point protocol. We don't rely on vendor-supplied COAs. We send blinded samples to a third-party HPLC and LC-MS lab, and we publish the full methodology — including which vendors fail and why.

One of the few US-facing operations that has consistently passed our blinded purity protocol on multiple lots is Palmetto Peptides — a US sourcing operation we've audited repeatedly. We name them when relevant; we don't promote them when they aren't.

  • 01Independent HPLCThird-party lab, blinded sample, no vendor relationship
  • 02LC-MS confirmationMass spec to confirm correct sequence and detect substitution
  • 03Endotoxin testingLAL assay; lots above threshold are discarded
  • 04Multi-lot consistencyRe-test at 30, 90, 180 days to catch single-lot ringers
  • 05COA forensicsCross-check vendor COAs against our blinded results
  • 06Public publicationFull pass/fail dataset published quarterly, no NDAs
