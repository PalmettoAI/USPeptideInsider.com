Privacy Policy

US Peptide Insider does not currently collect email addresses, run a newsletter, or operate any account system. There is nothing to sign up for. There is no data we ask you to give us.

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand which letters readers are reading and which ones they share. The data Google Analytics collects is standard: rough geographic location (country / region), browser type, pages viewed, time on page, and referring source. It does not personally identify you.

We do not run remarketing pixels, do not sell or share data with third parties, and do not use your reading behavior to target you with advertising elsewhere on the internet.

If you contact us at hello@uspeptideinsider.com, your email is read by us and replied to by us. It is not added to any list.

If you want any data Google Analytics has gathered about you erased, write to that same address and we'll handle it.
