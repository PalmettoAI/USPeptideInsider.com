CJC-1295 + Ipamorelin: the pulsatile GH stack, evaluated against the actual literature

CJC-1295 is a long-acting GHRH analog. Ipamorelin is a selective ghrelin receptor agonist. Stacked, they're the most-prescribed off-label growth hormone axis intervention in the US underground pharmacopoeia. The question is not whether they raise GH and IGF-1 — they do. The question is whether the magnitude justifies the cost, the injection burden, and the sourcing risk.

M. Aoyagi, Ph.D. / Updated 04.19.26 / 23 indexed studies / 14 min read
CJC-1295 + Ipamorelin Tetrasubstituted GHRH(1-29) · His-D-2-Nal-Ala-Trp-D-Phe-Lys-NH2

The CJC-1295 / Ipamorelin combination is what most physicians and pharmacists mean when they talk about "growth hormone secretagogue therapy." The pairing exists because each peptide acts on a different receptor system in the somatotrophic axis — and the combination produces a markedly larger and more physiologic GH pulse than either alone.

Mechanism, dosing, side-effect profile, and the actual sourcing landscape — including the small set of vendors we'd accept material from — follow. Stacking interactions live in the broader peptide stacking protocols guide; for the broader audit methodology that informs the sourcing recommendations below, see our 2026 sourcing guide.

Mechanism: two receptor systems, one pulse

CJC-1295 is a synthetic analog of growth hormone-releasing hormone (GHRH). The four amino acid substitutions in its sequence (D-Ala, Gln, Ala, Leu) protect it from dipeptidyl peptidase IV degradation and meaningfully extend its half-life. The DAC-modified version (CJC-1295 with Drug Affinity Complex, the version with the maleimide that binds albumin) has a half-life on the order of days; the non-DAC version, on the order of hours.

Ipamorelin is a selective agonist of the growth hormone secretagogue receptor (GHS-R1a) — the same receptor activated endogenously by ghrelin. Unlike older GHRPs (GHRP-2, GHRP-6), ipamorelin produces minimal cortisol or prolactin elevation in human studies (PMID 9849822), which is the principal reason it has displaced earlier GHRPs in field protocols.

When co-administered, GHRH-analog activity (CJC) and ghrelin-mimetic activity (ipamorelin) act synergistically at the somatotroph. Combined administration produces approximately 2–3x the GH peak of either agent alone, with retention of the natural pulsatile pattern — which matters because exogenous recombinant GH (somatropin) suppresses pulsatility, while secretagogue stacks largely preserve it.

Clinical evidence

The evidence base for CJC-1295 and ipamorelin individually is meaningfully larger than for BPC-157 — both have been studied in registrational-quality trials, though primarily for indications other than the body-composition use that dominates the off-label market.

  • CJC-1295 Phase II in growth hormone deficiency demonstrated dose-dependent IGF-1 elevation sustained for 7+ days post-injection at the DAC dose (PMID 16352683).
  • Ipamorelin Phase II trials in postoperative ileus and elective abdominal surgery demonstrated tolerability and GH-releasing activity, with the cortisol/prolactin selectivity that distinguishes it from GHRP-2.
  • The combination protocol that dominates field practice has not been studied in a registrational trial. The synergy is well-characterized in pharmacology; the clinical body-composition outcomes are extrapolated from individual-agent data.

The DAC variant simplifies injection burden but produces sustained, non-pulsatile elevations that more closely resemble exogenous GH dosing — which most secretagogue protocols are explicitly trying to avoid. The non-DAC version is the protocol-conformant choice when pulsatility is the goal.

Common protocol error Co-administered with food, particularly carbohydrates, ipamorelin's GH pulse is materially blunted. Ghrelin signaling at GHS-R1a is glucose-suppressed. Inject in a fasted state, ideally 90+ minutes after the last carbohydrate-containing meal.

Sourcing — and why this stack has the worst signal-to-noise problem in peptides

CJC-1295 and ipamorelin are among the most counterfeited peptides in the US-facing market. The reasons:

  1. Both are relatively expensive to synthesize honestly — 29-residue (CJC) and 5-residue (ipamorelin) peptides have meaningful cost floors.
  2. Both can be substituted with shorter, cheaper peptides that produce similar reconstitution behavior and won't be detected without LC-MS.
  3. The user typically cannot perceive a quality difference because GH/IGF-1 elevations are not subjectively obvious in the first 4–6 weeks.

This is a category where third-party COA verification matters more than for almost any other peptide. The vendor audit methodology details the LC-MS protocol used to detect substitution; the published quarterly dataset names which vendors have passed and which have failed.

For a US-based reader who would rather not run their own audit, we recommend filtering vendors by: (a) US jurisdiction, (b) per-lot HPLC and LC-MS published, (c) endotoxin testing, (d) consistent multi-lot history. Side effects and risk profile

  • Injection-site reactions — common, transient, particularly with non-DAC formulations
  • Water retention / mild edema — IGF-1-mediated, dose-dependent, usually resolves in 2–4 weeks
  • Carpal tunnel symptoms — occasionally reported, more common at supraphysiologic IGF-1 levels
  • Insulin sensitivity degradation — modest, reversible on cessation, more pronounced with DAC variant
  • Theoretical proliferative concern — IGF-1 elevation in the context of any active malignancy is contraindicated

The cortisol/prolactin selectivity of ipamorelin is the major safety advantage relative to GHRP-2 and GHRP-6, which both produce notable cortisol elevations and HPA-axis perturbation at typical doses. We've covered the older GHRP literature in the broader GH-axis section of the database.

Buyer's checklist

  • Are you sourcing CJC-1295 (no-DAC) or CJC-1295-DAC? Confirm which on the COA — the products are not interchangeable.
  • Has the vendor published LC-MS spectra showing the correct mass (≈ 3,367 Da for CJC-1295 no-DAC; ≈ 711 Da for ipamorelin)?
  • Is the price plausible? Real CJC-1295 has a synthesis cost; sub-$20 5mg vials are functionally always substituted material.
  • Is endotoxin testing published? Both peptides are typically reconstituted for SubQ injection.
  • Does the vendor sell the no-DAC form separately? Avoid combo vials at random ratios; you want dosing precision.
