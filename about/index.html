Editorial

Methodology, board, and the things we'd rather you knew

We are not a vendor. We are not a clinic. We are a small editorial operation that audits the US-facing peptide market and publishes the results — the pass-fail data, the methodology behind it, and the disclosures that exist on every other peptide site only in the FAQ footer in 9-point gray.

Editorial board

The standing board includes researchers in synthetic peptide chemistry, analytical chemistry (HPLC / LC-MS), endocrinology, and integrative medicine. Each member's relevant publications and any vendor relationships are published on the board roster. None hold equity in any peptide vendor we audit.

Vendor audit methodology

The full audit methodology is on the sourcing guide page: blinded purchase, third-party HPLC, LC-MS confirmation, endotoxin testing, multi-lot consistency, COA forensics, and quarterly publication of the full dataset. Vendors are not informed in advance and do not receive results before publication.

Affiliate and revenue disclosure

This site is reader-supported and earns revenue from three sources: (1) reader subscriptions to the quarterly vendor brief, (2) referral fees from a small number of named, audited vendors, and (3) sale of independently produced research reports.

Affiliate revenue does not influence the audit methodology, scoring, or pass/fail determination. We have, on multiple occasions, scored non-affiliated vendors above affiliated ones. Where we recommend a specific vendor in an article, the recommendation is contextual to the audit data, not the affiliate relationship.

Contact

Editorial inquiries, audit submissions, and corrections: research@uspeptideinsider.com. We acknowledge correspondence within 72 hours and publish dated correction notes at the top of any affected entry.
